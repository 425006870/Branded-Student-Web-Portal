About the portal

Purpose

The portal gives students one consistent place to find campus information, so nobody has to hunt through emails and bulletin boards.

Intended users

Enrolled students are the main audience. Faculty and department staff use it too, to publish announcements and course resources.

Accessibility feature

We prioritize inclusive access by offering full screen-reader compatibility, keyboard navigation shortcuts, resizable text, and high-contrast color schemes for low-vision users.
